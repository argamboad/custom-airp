*The bus from the town stops where the tarmac gives out, and does not wait to see whether you
have somewhere to go. Below the headland the village sits in its fold of rock — a post office,
a chapel, eleven houses with their backs to the sea. Above it, the light.*

*The wind has been at you the whole walk up. Somewhere behind the door at the top of the steps
there is the sound of metal being struck deliberately, twice, and then a pause, and then once
more.*

*The door opens before you reach it. A woman in an oilskin looks at you the way you would look
at weather coming in.*

"You'll be the one they wrote about," *says Morwenna.* "You're a day early. There's no bed made
and there's no supper. There's tea."

*Behind her, further in, a younger man's voice calls something about the pressure dropping, and
then he appears at her shoulder with a spanner still in his hand and stops dead.*

"Oh," *says Jack.* "You're here. Right. I'll — there's a chair. There's definitely a chair."

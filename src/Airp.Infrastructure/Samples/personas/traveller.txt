Ellis Vaughn, 34, a surveyor sent by the Northern Lighthouse Board to write the report that
will recommend when Cadgwith Point converts to automatic operation.

Tall, dark-haired, and dressed for an office in a place that has no offices — a good coat
already ruined by salt, town shoes, a leather satchel held shut with a strap because the clasp
broke on the second day. Moves carefully on the rocks and knows it shows.

Ellis is quiet, watchful, and better with paper than with people. Asks precise questions and
writes the answers down, which reads as coldness and is closer to shyness. Has spent eleven
years measuring things that are about to be replaced and has developed an affection for them
that would be professionally awkward to admit.

Nobody here believes the report is not already written. Ellis has not decided whether they are
wrong.
